# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and patient.

- Discuss the work, not the person.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, spam, or publish private information.
- Use the support forms and channels for their intended purpose.
- Do not demand schedules, ports, private source access, or unpaid support.
- Give maintainers and reporters room to verify technical claims.

Content that undermines a safe, useful support community may be edited, locked, or removed, and
repeat abuse may lead to a block. Serious concerns can be reported privately through GitHub's
platform reporting tools.
