# Realistic Trading Overhaul

Realistic Trading Overhaul (RTO) replaces vanilla's trading screen with a cart. You add as many
units of a merchant's offers as you like and buy them all with one Confirm. Prices, stock, villager
XP, level-ups, statistics and advancements work exactly as if you had made each trade in vanilla.

Operators also get an in-game **trade editor** that changes which trades villagers and wandering
traders draw, saved as a world datapack.

## What RTO does not change

- Offers come from vanilla's trade pools, or from pools an operator changed with the editor.
- There is no custom currency, village economy, list view, search or favourites.
- Without editor changes, the game plays exactly like vanilla apart from the cart screen.

Official binaries are distributed only on CurseForge. This support project hosts no JARs and no mod
source code.
