# Commands

| Command | Who | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/rto editor` | Operators at the configured permission level | Opens the [trade editor](Trade-Editor) |

Players need no command: the cart screen opens when you trade with a villager.

Vanilla commands for the editor's datapack:

```text
/datapack list enabled
/datapack enable "file/realistictradingoverhaul_trades.zip"
/datapack disable "file/realistictradingoverhaul_trades.zip"
```
