# Modpack permission

You may include an unmodified Realistic Trading Overhaul file in a CurseForge modpack when
CurseForge supplies it through the project's approved dependency and distribution systems.

## Allowed

- Add the RTO CurseForge project as a modpack dependency.
- Configure RTO for the pack or server.
- Link players to the official CurseForge project and this Wiki.

## Not allowed

- Upload the RTO JAR inside a modpack archive.
- Rehost the JAR on GitHub, a website, file-sharing service, launcher CDN, or another mod platform.
- Distribute a modified, translated, decompiled, or derivative JAR.
- Sell RTO or claim it as your own work.

For a distribution model not covered here, request permission through a public support issue without
attaching or requesting private source.

The full controlling terms are in the repository
[LICENSE](https://github.com/Reality-Craft-Interactive/realistic-trading-overhaul-support/blob/main/LICENSE).
